Reference proofs

Drop a font and compare it with Google Fonts families of the same script: one row per character, then text pages where the font sits next to three references at a time. Print the result, or save it as a PDF. The font you drop stays in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

1 · Font to review

Drop a TTF, OTF or WOFF file, or click to chooseA variable font is fine.
or use a family from Google Fonts

2 · Compare with

Each column is one named instance of the font (fvar); the highlighted one is the default location. A quick way to check every glyph across the design space.

3 · Proof

Landscape A4. In the print dialog, turn on “Background graphics” if the highlighted column comes out white.
Drop a font to start.

Reference families are read from google/fonts (main branch). Suggested sets are in site/reference-proofs/data/reference_sets.json. Sample texts come from gflanguages.
